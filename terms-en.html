What the App does

The App fetches the results of publicly available numerical pressure calculations and handles the turning points of pressure (the start of a fall and the turn back up) and your condition logs on your device. There is no user registration, and your records are stored only on your device.

The pressure values shown are results of numerical calculations published by MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute). They are not predictions made by the App, and they are not a weather forecast. The values contain errors and may differ from pressure actually measured. Some time ranges may be unavailable because of the provider.
