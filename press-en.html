Blog blurb

KIATSU (Pressure on Your Side) is an iPhone app that lines up numerical pressure values with your own symptom log. It is not a weather-forecast app, and it is not intended to diagnose or treat illness. Free features include records, notices, and the 48-hour view. Travel mode’s point is capturing pressure changes from the lay of the land, or elevators and other up-and-down moves — not only the graph you see when still — and showing the weather share versus the height share on the breakdown card. Recording can continue with the screen off; sudden shifts can notify you. Placebo-free mode hides pressure on Home and History for 10 days so you log how you feel only. Pressure notices pause. Afterwards you can compare your log with how pressure moved, and apply clear findings so notice timing and week-strip marks match you. The annual plan (7-day free trial) unlocks the 7-day graph, week strip, and an export that is easier to share with a clinician. Cancel during the trial and you will not be charged. Pressure values are numerical model output from MET Norway. They contain errors and may differ from measured pressure.
