KIATSU: Pressure on Your Side

Work with pressure. Find your better hours.

How air pressure ties into how you feel differs from person to person. KIATSU lines up those changes with your own log so you can learn your pattern and put pressure on your side — find hours that suit you, and place your plans on your own terms.

Once you know your pattern, you can put focused work into the hours that suit you, and shape plans around the phases you care about. KIATSU helps you work with the pressure instead of being pushed around by it.

Pressure while you sit still is not the whole story. What you feel from the lay of the land, or going up and down in an elevator, can come from height — not from the weather. Travel mode captures those pressure changes and splits the weather share from the height share on the breakdown card.

As your log grows, you can apply what you see to “Pressure and how you feel”. Then notice timing and the marks on the week strip line up with your own records.

What you can do

Travel mode: capture pressure changes from the land and from going up or down

  • Record pressure changes from the lay of the land, or elevators and other up-and-down moves — separately from the graph you see when still
  • The breakdown card shows the weather share and the height share side by side
  • Recording continues in another app or with the screen off, and sudden shifts can notify you with a cause hint

The current phase and the next turn

  • See the current pressure phase (falling, rising, turning, stable) and the time until the next turn
  • Pressure graph for the next 48 hours, overlaid with your past records
  • Choose the phases that tend to affect you and get a notice 1, 3 or 6 hours before they start

Your log

  • Log how you feel and look back on your history

Placebo-free mode: log without seeing pressure, then compare

  • For 10 days, hide pressure on Home and History and log how you feel only
  • Pressure notices pause; record reminders at morning and evening still arrive
  • Afterwards, compare your log with how pressure moved

Line up notices and display from your log

  • Apply what Compare shows to “Pressure and how you feel”
  • Notice timing before tougher phases then follows that pattern
  • Week-strip marks update the same way (the annual plan also uses it for a suggested better window)

How to use it

  1. Choose the phases that tend to affect you
  2. Get a notice before those phases and adjust plans if you want
  3. Log how you feel and line it up with pressure changes
  4. For the land around you or elevators and other up-and-down moves, use Travel mode to capture height-driven pressure changes and split them from weather
  5. To reduce guesswork, use Placebo-free mode and compare afterwards
  6. Apply what you learned so notice timing and the week strip match you

FAQ

Is this a weather forecast app?
No. Pressure values are numerical model output from MET Norway. They are not a forecast, and this app does not make its own predictions.
Does the app decide when I will feel worse?
No. You choose the phases that tend to affect you and check your own pattern with your log. It is not for diagnosis or treatment.
What stays free?
Records, notices, the 48-hour view, Travel mode, and Placebo-free mode are free. The 7-day graph, week strip, and export are on the annual plan.
How do I cancel the annual plan?
Use Settings › your name › Subscriptions on iPhone, or open the same place from the app settings. Cancel during the trial and you will not be charged.
Do my records move when I change phones?
No. Records stay on the device. You can restore the annual plan with Restore purchases.
I am not getting notices.
Check system notification permission and the in-app phase and notice settings. Nothing is sent while no chosen phase is ahead. During Placebo-free mode, pressure notices pause.
Why is there a Travel mode?
Pressure while you sit still does not tell you whether a change from the land or an elevator was weather or height. Travel mode captures those changes and shows the weather share and the height share on the breakdown card.
Do notices and the display follow my records?
Yes. When you apply what Compare shows to “Pressure and how you feel”, notice timing before tougher phases and the week-strip marks line up with that pattern. You can also choose the pattern yourself in Settings.
What is location used for?
Fetching pressure for where you are, and recording pressure and height changes in Travel mode. Outside Travel mode, location is not collected in the background.
Can I share records with a clinic?
The annual plan can export records in a form that is easier to hand over. It does not replace medical advice.

No account needed

There is no sign-up and no login. Your records are stored only on your device. The developer runs no server, and your symptom records never leave your device. To fetch pressure values, the app sends the data provider a location rounded to about 1 km, plus the app name, version and a contact address (the provider's terms require apps to identify themselves). When you search for a city, the search text is sent too. No ads and no usage analytics.

Please note

This app is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any disease, and it does not replace advice from a doctor. If you are worried about your health, please consult a medical professional.

Pressure values are numerical model output published by MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute). They are not a weather forecast, and this app does not make its own predictions. Values contain errors and may differ from measured pressure.
